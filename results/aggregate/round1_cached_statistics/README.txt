
Leave-one-disease-out tables enumerate every represented reference diagnosis within each fixed comparison population. The methods table gives AP; the contrasts table gives left-minus-right AP and its sign relative to the undeleted panel. Blank differences retain undefined endpoints. These are descriptive estimates with no added significance tests. The core table orients selected contrasts consistently with the manuscript. The correct-proposal DDXPlus common population represents 45 diseases; the broader study includes 46. TRACE complete_only and include_partial_exact remain separate.

Mapping-change effects use complete adjudicated references and paired probe availability, with no full-input-correctness restriction. Citation additions/removals classify the correction pattern; they do not assign linguistic error causes. Audit strata overlap in DDXPlus and remain separate. Score differences use method-specific finite pairs, with their counts reported. Natural binary-outcome pair concordances are descriptive; multiple pairs from one patient are not independent observations.
