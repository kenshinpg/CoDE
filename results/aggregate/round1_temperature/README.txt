Development-temperature sensitivity. development_temperatures.csv records positive scalar temperatures fitted by development NLL. The pooled temperature equally weights budgets 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1; per-budget fits are diagnostics. test_calibration.csv and test_reliability_bins.csv give the fixed test populations and ten confidence bins. core_rankings_paired_intervals.csv reports paired original, calibrated and change estimates, with 5,000 cluster draws per dataset/RQ family and simultaneous intervals over its three contrasts. Coverage scores and hard outcomes are fixed, so coverage-minus-group contrasts are identical by construction. The common family radius can give nonzero simultaneous intervals even for such identically zero changes; their marginal change intervals are [0,0]. rq2_descriptive_rankings.csv includes other proposal strata and probes. AP differences are in AP units; controlled concordance differences are fractions (multiply by 100 for percentage points). Intervals condition on fixed fitted models and development temperatures. These are sensitivity results; original manuscript tables retain the original probabilities.
